About the show

About

The KCA Awards is a fan-voted award show for the K-pop cover community on TikTok, celebrating singers, groups, and entertainments. Winners are revealed live on Discord.

The road to the show

How it works

  1. Nominee applicationsSingers and groups apply themselves with their TikTok.
  2. VerificationMods check that each TikTok belongs to the person who applied.
  3. Round 1 voteVoters pick favorites from every nominee in each category.
  4. Finalists announcedThe top 3 by votes plus 1–2 judges' picks in every category.
  5. Final voteOne vote per category. Results stay hidden until the show.
  6. The showWinners are revealed live in the Discord Stage.

Read before you apply

Rules

Requirements

  • At least 10 videos posted on your TikTok.
  • At least 100 followers on your TikTok.
  • You've debuted or released at least one cover. Entertainments need at least one debuted group or released cover.

Applying

  • Singers and groups: singing and rap covers and original songs only. Dance-only videos aren't eligible.
  • Entertainments: your entertainment must be active on TikTok within the eligibility window.
  • Your cover must be posted on TikTok within the eligibility window.
  • It must be your own work. Stolen or reposted content is disqualified.
  • Apply for as many categories as you like.
  • Groups apply once, through one member. Entertainments apply once, through an owner.
  • Sign in with Google, Discord, or TikTok to apply or nominate, and use a clear, readable name.
  • Photos and logos must be appropriate. Mods can hide any application.
  • You must be 13 or older.

Voting

  • You need the Voter role to vote. It goes to members whose Discord account is 30+ days old and who have been in the server 7+ days.
  • One vote per person, per category.
  • Nominees can vote, including for themselves.
  • Alt accounts, vote trading, or asking people to make accounts to vote gets the nominee removed.
  • Mods check every vote against the Voter role list before counting.

Questions

FAQ

Who can apply?

Any singer, group, or entertainment in the K-pop cover community on TikTok that is in the KCA Discord.

What is an entertainment?

A fan-run K-pop company on TikTok that holds auditions and debuts its own groups. Entertainments compete in their own awards.

What is the intro video?

An optional video showing why you should win, like a compilation of your best covers or moments. Voters see it on your applicant card.

What do I need to apply?

At least 10 TikTok videos, at least 100 followers, and at least one debut or released cover. Mods check this when they verify you.

Does it cost anything?

No. Applying and voting are free.

Can I apply for more than one category?

Yes, as many as you like. Add the right cover for each one.

Can I apply with a cover I haven't posted yet?

Yes, as long as it goes up on TikTok within the eligibility window.

How do I vote?

Get the Voter role in the Discord. Voting links are posted there when each round opens.

How are winners picked?

A round 1 fan vote picks the top 3 in each category, judges add 1–2 picks, then a final fan vote decides the winner.

How do I apply?

Sign in, fill out the application, and add your photo or logo. You show up on the Applicants page and in the KCA Discord list right away.

When do applications open?

Every season opens on September 1. Applications close on the date shown on the home page.

When do I get the verified check?

Once a mod sees the code in your TikTok bio. Keep it there until the check shows up on your card.

What happens after the show?

Applications, photos, and nominations are cleared for the next season. Winners stay in the Hall of Fame forever.

Privacy

The KCA Awards is a fan-run award show for the K-pop cover community. This page explains what the site collects and why.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google, Discord, or TikTok, we receive your account ID, display name, and email if the platform shares it. We use this only to keep your application and nominations tied to you.
  • If you apply, we store what you enter: your public name, pronunciation, TikTok, socials, links to your videos, your photo or logo, and your Discord username.
  • If you nominate someone, we store your name, the nominee, and your reason.

Who sees it

  • Your application is public on the Applicants page and in the KCA Discord list, except your Discord username and email, which only mods can see.
  • Nominations are only visible to mods.
  • We never sell your information or show ads.

How long we keep it

  • Everything is deleted when the season ends, except winners, who stay in the Hall of Fame.
  • You can withdraw your application any time from the Apply page, which deletes it and your photo.
  • To delete your account or ask a question, message a mod in the KCA Discord.

The KCA Awards

Terms

  • The KCA Awards is a free, fan-run event. It isn't connected to TikTok, YouTube, Google, Discord, or any K-pop company.
  • You must be 13 or older to sign in, apply, or nominate.
  • Only submit covers, photos, and logos that are yours or that you have permission to use.
  • Photos and names must be appropriate. Mods can hide or remove any application.
  • Alt accounts, vote trading, or harassment can get you removed from the awards.
  • Prizes are donated by community partners and may change.
  • These terms can change between seasons. Check this page before you apply.
